About Us

Our guest house is more than just a place to stay — it’s a home away from home. Designed with a blend of modern architecture and traditional Ghanaian hospitality, we provide a serene environment for travelers, families, and professionals alike. From the moment you step through our doors, you’ll be greeted with warmth, personalized service, and attention to detail that ensures your stay is memorable.

Whether you’re visiting Accra for business meetings, cultural exploration, or simply to relax, our guest house offers the perfect balance of convenience and tranquility. With spacious rooms, lush gardens, and easy access to the city’s vibrant attractions, we make every guest feel valued and at ease.

Our Services

  • Guest Rooms: Elegant rooms with air conditioning, Wi-Fi, and private balconies.
  • Spa & Wellness: Relax with massages, facials, and wellness treatments.
  • Dining: Enjoy freshly prepared meals featuring both local Ghanaian dishes and international cuisine.
  • Bar & Lounge: A cozy space to unwind with cocktails, wines, and local drinks.
  • Conference Facilities: Fully equipped meeting rooms for business events and seminars.
  • Airport Shuttle: Convenient transport to and from Kotoka International Airport.

Nearby Attractions

Located in the heart of Accra, our guest house offers easy access to some of the city’s most exciting attractions:

  • Kwame Nkrumah Mausoleum: A historic site celebrating Ghana’s first president.
  • Makola Market: A bustling marketplace for shopping and cultural experiences.
  • Labadi Beach: Perfect for relaxation, music, and seaside fun.
  • Accra Mall: Modern shopping and entertainment center.
